## Enrolment open for the 2026/2027 course

Enrolment is now open for the 2026/2027 course at the Sociedad Unión Lírica Pinosense Music School.

Returning students can renew their enrolment through the following form:

[Returning student re-enrolment form](https://forms.gle/912WGa8RZv1kjaMp9)

New students should go to the Music School office during opening hours to complete enrolment and receive the necessary information.

You can also check all the detailed information in the following document:

[View enrolment information for the 2026/2027 course](/web/pdfs/matricula-curso-26-27.pdf)

We also recommend checking the school calendar for the 2026/2027 course in the corresponding section of this page.
